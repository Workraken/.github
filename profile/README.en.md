<h1 align="center">More order in your work. More time to grow.</h1>

<h3><strong>Everything you need to work with more clarity and less hassle.</strong></h3>

<p><strong>WorKraken</strong> brings together your day-to-day operations, your customer relationships and a brand that showcases everything you do.</p>

<h2>🧭 We make running your business simpler</h2>

- **Everything in one place:** organise customers, payments, expenses, tasks and results without switching between tools.
- **Better support for your customers:** keep every conversation and follow-up connected to your daily operations.
- **A brand that represents you:** build a professional identity and a digital presence that reflect the value of your work.
- **Less repetitive work:** automate everyday tasks and free up time for what matters.
- **Information that helps you decide:** understand what is happening through clear, straightforward dashboards and reports.
- **Your business, always within reach:** access and manage the information you need on the web or your phone.

<h2>🛠️ Tools for your everyday work</h2>

- **WorKraken web:** the hub of your operations. Manage rentals, car parks, fleets, expenses and financial balances in one place.
- **WorKraken mobile:** a fast, practical way to keep track of your business wherever you are.
